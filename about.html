About SpaceFleet

Our purpose

SpaceFleet is an educational space discovery portal. The goal is to make common space topics easier to understand and to help visitors find established resources for live or specialist information.

What you can explore

  • Satellite orbits, tracking concepts and satellite passes.
  • International Space Station background and viewing resources.
  • Space weather concepts including solar flares and geomagnetic storms.
  • Space mission categories and ways to research missions.
  • Guidance for finding and evaluating current space news.

How we use external resources

Some links on SpaceFleet lead to NASA, NOAA, tracking services or publishers operated by other organizations. Those sites control their own content and data. SpaceFleet does not claim ownership of external material and does not present an external service as part of SpaceFleet.

Editorial approach

Our pages are written to explain concepts rather than simply redirect visitors. When information is time-sensitive, readers are encouraged to verify it with the relevant primary source.
