What is a satellite orbit?

An orbit is the curved path an object follows around another body under gravity. Altitude, inclination and velocity determine how a satellite moves around Earth.

Low Earth orbit satellites complete an orbit relatively quickly, while higher orbits can take much longer. Different orbits are used for communications, Earth observation, navigation and science.

What is TLE data?

Two-Line Element data is a standardized representation used by tracking software to describe an object's orbit. Tracking systems use orbital data and mathematical models to estimate positions.
