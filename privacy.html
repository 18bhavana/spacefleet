Privacy Policy

Overview

SpaceFleet ("we," "us," or "our") operates spacefleet.in. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the website and how it may be used.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information contained in your message, including your email address and any information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your enquiry, feedback, correction, or collaboration request.

Analytics

SpaceFleet may use website analytics in the future to understand traffic, page usage, performance, and general audience trends. If analytics are enabled, the relevant analytics provider may collect information such as device or browser information, approximate location, pages viewed, and usage events, subject to that provider's policies and your applicable privacy choices.

If we add an analytics service, this policy will be updated to identify the service and describe its relevant data practices.

Cookies and similar technologies

SpaceFleet currently does not intentionally require cookies for basic browsing. If we introduce analytics, advertising, preferences, or other technologies that use cookies or local storage, this policy will be updated to describe their purposes and the relevant third parties.

Advertising

SpaceFleet may display advertising in the future, including Google AdSense or other advertising partners. If advertising is introduced, third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, or personalize advertising as permitted by applicable settings and law.

When advertising is enabled, this policy will be updated with the applicable advertising partners and links to relevant privacy or opt-out information.

Consent and privacy choices

Where applicable, SpaceFleet will use an appropriate consent mechanism for cookies, advertising, analytics, or other processing that requires user consent. Consent settings may vary by region and by the services enabled on the website.

External websites

SpaceFleet links to third-party websites such as space agencies, tracking services, news publishers, and other resources. Those websites have their own privacy policies and data practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Data retention

Email enquiries may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain correspondence, address corrections, or keep appropriate business records.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SpaceFleet adds services such as analytics, advertising, cookies, or contact forms. The latest version will be published on this page.
